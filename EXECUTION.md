# Execution and data contract

These are specifications, not broker-connected programs. All defaults, costs, risk limits and parameter bounds are unvalidated research choices. A strategy is eligible for simulation only after every required input and indicator is implemented. Do not fill missing data or unavailable borrow with invented values.

## Clock, bars and evaluation
- t is the last fully completed bar. O,H,L,C,V are its open/high/low/close/base-unit traded volume. [k] means k completed bars ago. All rolling calculations include t unless [1], “preceding”, “formation”, or “excluding t” is specified. “Preceding n” means t-n through t-1.
- Evaluate entry and discretionary exit conditions once at bar close. Submit at the next available bar open. No use of final daily close for an order retrospectively filled at that same close. “Crosses above” means x[t]>y[t] AND x[t-1]<=y[t-1]; crosses below reverses the inequalities. A fixed threshold is a constant series. Plain conditions are level conditions and can recur while flat.
- US stocks/ETFs: regular session only, official exchange calendar and America/New_York timezone including DST. Daily bars are exchange sessions. 5-minute bars are aligned to 09:30 local and labeled by their closing timestamp. Crypto: a single preselected exchange and quote currency; daily bars start 00:00 UTC, weekly bars Monday 00:00 UTC. Do not mix exchange prices or volume.
- Multi-timeframe rules use only higher-timeframe bars completed by t. A crypto UTC week is not a trailing seven-day rolling bar. Monthly rules are evaluated at the last daily close of the month, with fills next session/day open. The 1mo strategy uses completed monthly bars, but retains daily or finer data for stop monitoring.
- Numeric entry times such as 22:00 UTC refer to a decision on the just-completed bar and execution at the immediately following bar open at that timestamp. Scheduled exits are submitted in advance and filled at the stated open, never based on future bar closes.
- All US 5-minute strategies are flat overnight: pre-schedule liquidation at the open of the last regular-session 5-minute bar (15:55 on a full day, adjusted on early closes). Cancel all new entries at/after the earlier of 15:00 and 60 minutes before session close. If the stated entry window is absent because of an early close, skip. Crypto and daily holiday rules are not subject to this liquidation.

## Protective stops, target and event order
- At entry, freeze E=actual fill and A=ATR(14) from the signal bar. For ATR profiles initial stop S=E-stop_atr*A; target T=E+take_profit_atr*A. Skip if A<=0 or S<=0. Neither level moves. Use reduce-only OCO orders where supported; emulate cautiously otherwise. “Stop always active” is an order policy, not a guaranteed execution price.
- Stops and targets become active immediately after fill. Long stop at a gap-down open fills at open less modeled slippage, not the old stop. A resting long target limit on a favorable gap fills conservatively at target (never below its limit). Intrabar stop touches fill at stop less slippage. Charge costs on every fill.
- If the same OHLC bar touches stop and target and ordering cannot be resolved with finer data, assume stop first. Report this ambiguity. Same entry-bar stop/target tests are permitted because entry is at its open; do not assume a target happened before the stop.
- At an open, process forced/scheduled liquidation and already-triggered exits before new entries. A gap through a stop has priority over a discretionary exit at the same open. If flat after exit, do not re-enter on that same bar. Require at least one new fully completed flat bar before another signal.
- max_candles counts complete held bars starting with the entry bar as 1. After bar N closes, exit at next open if still held; do not count the signal bar. All listed signal exits are OR conditions, not requirements to wait for profit. An indicator exit can realize a loss.
- No pyramiding, averaging down, martingale, partial profit-taking, or automatic leverage. A default single-instrument strategy holds one position total; example tickers are alternatives to test separately, not permission to buy all simultaneously.

## Sizing and portfolio sleeves
- Allocate a separate capital sleeve per strategy before simulation. Do not give 120 strategies independent claims on the same account equity. Default single-position size q=floor_to_lot(min(risk_fraction*sleeve_equity/(E-S), sleeve_equity/E, participation_cap*trailing_mean_volume)). risk_fraction defaults to 0.005; participation_cap defaults to 0.01. Fractional-share/coin rounding depends on venue. Skip below minimum lot/notional. This is intended loss at stop, not a loss guarantee.
- Portfolio strategies have explicit target weights instead of single-position risk sizing. Long only, total weights<=100% of their sleeve; no leverage. Empty eligibility => cash. If fewer than k qualify, normalize across the eligible number unless the individual rule explicitly scales exposure or keeps fixed component allocations. Ranking ties resolve by ticker alphabetical order unless another rule is specified.
- Month-end rebalances fill at next daily open. Sell removed/overweight assets first, then buy to target using available settled buying power; proportional scaling if fees or liquidity leave insufficient cash. No hidden margin borrowing. Enforce participation and lot constraints. Leftover notional remains cash.
- Portfolio hard stop per holding is E*(1-stop_loss_pct/100), default 15%. Monitor on daily or finer bars even though selection is monthly. At partial reductions retain original E and age; at additions set E to weighted-average cost and do NOT lower the existing stop: new stop=max(old stop, new E*(1-stop_loss_pct/100)). Never reset age until fully flat. Stop exits cannot be reversed until the next month-end selection. max_candles=252 default triggers at next daily open; do not re-enter on that same execution bar. Month-end selection can admit it at a later scheduled rebalance.
- No profit target for allocation strategies: the sell/rebalance rule, per-holding protective stop and time cap govern exits. The stop/time overlay materially changes passive rebalancing and published allocation methods.

## Pair execution
- Each pair strategy holds at most one long-A/short-B pair, long-spread direction only. Both legs enter/exit together at synchronized next opens; if either leg is unavailable, skip. In live execution enforce an atomic spread order where possible, otherwise cancel/unwind immediately on partial-leg failure. Backtest this execution risk, not risk-free simultaneous fills.
- Set initial gross notional G<=100% sleeve equity. Equal dollars => long=short=G/2. Ratio 1:beta => long_A=G/(1+beta), short_B=beta*G/(1+beta); require finite beta>0. Freeze share/coin quantities after entry. This is a gross-unlevered notional constraint, not zero margin requirement or guaranteed market neutrality.
- Require borrow confirmation, broker margin permissions, collateral, fee schedule, dividend-in-lieu and recall handling before entry. Margin crypto is not the same as ordinary spot. Disable if those inputs are missing; do not replace a stock/spot short with a perpetual without rewriting funding and liquidation rules.
- Pair close-based protective loss: when cumulative net pair P&L including fees, carry and distributions <= -pair_stop_pct/100 * entry gross G, exit both at next synchronized open. It is not an intrabar guarantee. In addition always maintain per-leg hard stops: long E_A*(1-leg_stop_pct/100), short E_B*(1+leg_stop_pct/100), default 8%. A leg stop forces the other leg out at first executable price. Gaps and non-synchronous bars can exceed both limits. If within-bar path is unknown assume the adverse leg stop first and mark the other at its worst bar extreme plus costs; report this conservative convention.
- Time cap defaults to 40 daily bars. Formation/trading-window expiry and model-specific z stops are additional OR exits. Borrow recall or margin shortfall exits both legs immediately at the next available executable price. Track net and gross exposures separately.

## Data quality and costs
- Use corporate-action-consistent adjusted OHLC for indicators, with split-consistent volume. For actual fills use raw tradable prices and map signal levels/positions across splits. Monthly ranking and pair formation use dividend-reinvested total-return closes; daily oscillator/candle indicators use split-adjusted close without artificial cash-dividend reinvestment jumps. Keep separate signal/total-return/execution series and document adjustment factors; do not mix units inside one OHLC series.
- A total-return C is the default for portfolio ranking and pair signals; ratio-pair prices in formulas refer to that series. All TR inputs must be available through t, never revised using future distributions. An implementation must carry actual dividends for long shares and dividend-in-lieu for shorts, and map share/coin quantities to actual tradable units at entry.
- Benchmarks/reference assets require same-date aligned closes. No forward fill of halted or missing prices. Require all selection-universe members to have valid history; otherwise skip that rebalance and leave weights unchanged, except sell/delist risk events. Do not backfill ETFs/coins before listing; use point-in-time universes for broad-stock extensions, including delisted instruments. This library uses fixed illustrative universes and is not free of retrospective asset-selection bias.
- Warm-up: at least 5 times the largest recursive indicator period, plus nested rolling requirements and lag buffers; finite-window indicators must have their complete history. If unavailable do not trade. Portfolio covariance uses a common complete intersection of dates. Skip zero/invalid denominators and NaN/inf outputs.
- Cost scenarios are assumptions, not current quotes: stock/ETF per side commission 1 bp, half-spread 2 bps, adverse market/stop slippage 2 bps; crypto per side commission 10 bps, half-spread 5 bps, adverse market/stop slippage 5 bps. Limits pay commission and cannot fill worse than their limit. Scale all costs 2x and 4x in stress tests. Add actual market impact, taxes and FX conversion where applicable. The default participation cap is 1% of preceding 20-bar average volume per order; allocation uses daily volume. For 5-minute orders use same-slot prior 20-session average rather than daily volume.
- Pair borrow/collateral rates and cash yields must be supplied historically; no fabricated default zero. If unavailable do not run pairs or the excess-cash momentum rule. Other uninvested cash can be modeled at zero only as an explicitly labeled conservative scenario, with a separate actual-cash-yield benchmark. Include cash and distributions consistently.
- Stop maintenance must survive disconnects; stale feed, clock drift, rejected protective orders, missing borrow or missing prices disable new orders. Paper-trade and reconcile fills/positions before considering real deployment.

## Additional fixed conventions
- For non-portfolio entries, veto a signal if its strategy-specific close-based exit condition is already true; scheduled future exits do not veto otherwise-valid entries.
- One basis point (bp) is 0.01 percentage points. All cost rates apply to actual traded notional per side; model half-spread plus adverse slippage by shifting fills against the trade, then charge commission separately.
- Bare ticker comparisons (for example SPY>SMA(200)) mean that ticker close compared to its own moving average. A lettered price with a subscript identifies that instrument, never a cross-asset average.
